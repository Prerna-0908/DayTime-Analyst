My story

I got into data because I like finding the reason behind a number. A chart is only useful if it changes a decision, so I try to begin every project with the question someone would actually ask.

My work so far covers the full path: cleaning messy data, exploring it, building models where they help, and packaging the result as a dashboard, alert or app. My featured project automates that whole path, from raw data to emailed alerts.

I enjoy work where the answer needs to be clear to people who do not read code. That is why every project on this site is framed as a business question first.
