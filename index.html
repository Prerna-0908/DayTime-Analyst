👩‍💻 AI Data Analyst Intern — Excelerate (Team Lead)

Led a team building an AI system predicting learner dropouts & recommending opportunities.

My Pov:

This internship was the moment everything clicked for me — Python, machine learning, dashboards, teamwork… all of it. And as the team leader, I wasn't just contributing, I was coordinating, guiding, fixing, and delivering. We worked on a massive educational dataset with 8,500+ learner records, and turned it into an actual AI-powered system that could predict dropouts and recommend opportunities. It was intense. Challenging. And honestly? Kind of life-changing.

What I actually Did?

  • ✨ Data Cleaning, Validation & EDA in Google Colab (Python) — Google Colab became my second home. I used Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn) to clean and validate 8,500+ raw records, handle missing values, fix date inconsistencies, remove invalid entries, perform EDA to uncover behavior patterns, engineer new features like Application Speed Index, Engagement Momentum, and Future Readiness, and visualize trends, distributions, correlations, and churn indicators. I wasn't just coding — I was uncovering stories hiding inside the data.
  • ✨ Feature Engineering & ML Modeling — I built and evaluated 4 different ML models: Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, SVM. As a team, we selected Random Forest — it achieved a 🔥 93.43% accuracy, the highest and most stable across tests. It became the backbone of our predictive system.
  • ✨ Designed a Recommendation Engine — After predicting dropouts, I created a content-based recommendation system using cosine similarity, engagement-based metrics, and behavioral features. This system suggested the best opportunities for each learner — basically making the experience personalized instead of generic.
  • ✨ Created a Power BI Dashboard — Once the backend was rock solid, I visualized everything in Power BI: trend charts, churn probability visuals, engagement funnels, feature importance summaries, behavioral insights. I wanted the dashboard to feel intuitive, clean, and "tell the whole story at a glance."
  • ✨ Led the Entire Team — Coordinating with 3 teammates, assigning tasks, guiding the Python workflows, cross-checking code and analysis, ensuring model outputs were correct, finalizing the report, making sure deadlines were met. Leadership wasn't just about assigning tasks — it was about being the one everyone came to when something broke.

The Project We Built:

We created a dual-framework AI system that could predict which learners were at risk of dropping out, and recommend opportunities to re-engage them. Our work combined ML, behavioral analytics, problem-solving and strategic insight. It felt like building a mini EdTech intelligence engine.

What I Learned

This internship taught me that Python isn't just a skill — it's a superpower. That leading a team is equal parts empathy and execution. That dashboards can literally change how people see data. And that AI becomes meaningful only when you bring human thinking into it. I walked in as a student. I walked out as someone who knows how to build real systems, not just practice projects.

  • Led a team for AI-powered dashboard project
  • Developed anomaly detection insights
  • Managed workflow & task assignment
  • Presented findings to global leadership
