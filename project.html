How I approach a business problem

The same four moves sit behind every project below.

Frame the question

Turn a vague need into a question the data can answer, with a clear definition of success.

Prepare the data

Clean, validate and shape it, because every later step depends on trustworthy inputs.

Analyse and model

Explore patterns, track the right KPIs and use models only where they help.

Recommend and deliver

Package the answer as a dashboard, alert or app that people will actually use.
